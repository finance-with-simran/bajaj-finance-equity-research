# Bajaj Finance – Equity Research & Investment Analysis

## Overview
A comprehensive investment analysis of Bajaj Finance Ltd, built as part of a self-initiated finance portfolio project. The analysis covers 10 years of financial performance, segment-wise AUM breakdown, credit quality trends, and a comparison of management guidance versus actual results.

## Objective
To evaluate Bajaj Finance's financial health and growth trajectory through fundamental analysis, and to form an independent investment thesis based on historical performance and forward-looking indicators.

## Key Areas Covered
- 10-year historical financial performance (revenue, profitability, asset quality)
- Assets Under Management (AUM) by business segment
- Credit quality analysis (NPA trends, provisioning)
- Management guidance vs. actual performance tracking
- Investment thesis and conclusion

## Data Source
Financial data sourced from [Screener.in](https://www.screener.in)

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel (multi-tab financial modeling, formulas, formatting)

## Files
- `Bajaj Finance Project.xlsx` – Full workbook with financial data, segment analysis, and investment thesis

## Key Takeaways
- Bajaj Finance has shown consistent AUM growth over the 10-year period, driven by diversification across consumer, SME, and commercial lending segments
- Credit quality metrics (NPA ratios) remained relatively stable, reflecting disciplined underwriting practices
- Actual performance closely tracked management's forward guidance in most years, indicating strong execution and credible leadership communication
- Overall, the analysis supports a positive long-term investment outlook, supported by strong fundamentals and consistent growth in core lending segments
